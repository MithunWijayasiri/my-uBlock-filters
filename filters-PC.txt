! --- YouTube - Mixes and Playlists ---
! Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/uBlockOrigin/wiki/solutions/youtube/

! Hide Mix playlists from homepage
youtube.com##ytd-rich-item-renderer:has([href*="start_radio=1"])

! Hide Mix playlists from sidebar and related videos
youtube.com##ytd-compact-radio-renderer, :is(#related yt-lockup-view-model,ytd-compact-video-renderer):has([href*="&start_radio=1"])

! Hide Mix playlists from search results
youtube.com##ytd-radio-renderer, :is(ytd-search yt-lockup-view-model,ytd-video-renderer[is-search]):has([href*="&start_radio=1"][aria-label])

! Hide Mix playlists from video end cards
youtube.com##.ytp-videowall-still[data-is-mix="true"]

! Hide YouTube Music playlists from sidebar
youtube.com##ytd-compact-playlist-renderer:has([title^="YouTube"])

! Hide "Thanks" button below player
www.youtube.com###top-row.ytd-watch-metadata yt-button-view-model:has([aria-label="Thanks"])

! Hide "Download" button below player
www.youtube.com###top-row.ytd-watch-metadata ytd-download-button-renderer

! Hide "Clip" button below player

! --- YouTube - Shorts ---

! Hide Shorts from search results (keep only regular videos)
